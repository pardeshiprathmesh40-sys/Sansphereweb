Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

SANSPHERE operates the student mobile application and public web portal. We respect your privacy regarding all personal academic details and account telemetry collected during usage.

1. Information Collection

We collect essential account setup information (email, university/course background) and user-contributed content (uploaded notes, reviews, peer chat interactions) necessary for functioning platform operation.

2. Usage of Data

Data is utilized exclusively to maintain your profile state, calculate SanCoins reward allocations, enable peer discussions, and safeguard protected documents.

Terms & Conditions

By registering or utilizing SANSPHERE services, you agree to adhere to community standard terms regarding intellectual property and peer engagement.

1. Intellectual Property & Upload Integrity

Users must only publish academic content they hold legitimate copyright or permission to share. Plagiarized or copyrighted textbook dumps without license are subject to immediate removal.

2. SanCoins Policy

SanCoins represent platform-bound engagement points and carry no speculative monetary guarantees outside designated ecosystem utility features.

Refund Policy

Transactions involving Marketplace resources and SanCoin unlock mechanisms are final upon content access. If an uploaded document is verified to be corrupted or mislabeled, buyers can request a coin reversal within 24 hours via support.

Community Guidelines

SANSPHERE is dedicated to fostering an encouraging academic environment. Harassment, spamming in peer chats, uploading inappropriate non-educational files, or attempt to manipulate SanCoins will lead to profile suspension.
